About

Built by one person, checked by more than one

Miqyas is an independent project; not affiliated with any mosque, board, or financial institution. It exists because most zakat calculators either pick one madhhab silently or blur all of them together. This one asks first, and holds the line after.

Miqyas was created by Muhammad Taha, Fatima Waqas, and Zainib Waqas. The Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi’i, and Hanbali rule sets are presented for comparison. Review the Methodology page and consult a qualified scholar for personal guidance.

Miqyas is a calculation aid, not a fatwa. For anything the tool flags as uncertain or unresolved, speak with a qualified scholar directly.
