Miqyas - an instrument, not an opinion

Choose a madhhab. Then the reading is yours.

Miqyas implements four schools of zakat fiqh - Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali - as four separate rule sets. Pick one below. Once selected, every question and calculation in your session draws only from that school. No blending, no silent defaults.

You can compare the full rule sets first - see Methodology.
Why one at a time

A calculation is only sound inside one school

Nisab thresholds, hawl timing, jewelry treatment, and debt deduction differ across the four madhahib - sometimes sharply. Miqyas locks your selection for the whole session so a result can never quietly borrow a rule from a school you didn't choose. If you want a second opinion under a different madhhab, that's a new session, not a toggle.

Miqyas - a zakat instrument, not a fatwa. Consult a qualified scholar for unresolved cases.
