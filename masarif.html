Where the number goes

Masarif is a directory of organizations that distribute zakat, tagged by which of the eight classical categories in Surah At-Tawbah 9:60 their work supports. Miqyas does not process payments. Every entry sends you to the organization’s own donation or information page. The directory begins in Niagara Region and grows only after each listing is checked.

Before donating, confirm the organization’s current zakat policy and follow its instructions for designating your gift.
