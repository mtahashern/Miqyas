Privacy Policy

What happens to your numbers

Calculations happen in your browser. The figures you enter into the calculator — cash, gold and silver, debts, receivables; are never sent to a server. They exist only in your browser's memory for the current session and disappear when you close the tab or select a different madhhab.

Masarif submissions. If you submit an organization for review through the Masarif intake form, that contact information is stored so it can be reviewed manually. It is used only for that review; not shared, not sold.

Analytics. Basic, privacy-safe traffic analytics may be used to understand which pages get used (not who's using them). See the Cookie Policy for exactly what that sets in your browser.

No accounts, no history, no tracking of individual results. There is currently no login system and no saved calculation history; each session is self-contained.

This page will be expanded if account features or saved history are added in a future version; until then, this is the complete policy.
